# 🛠️ ChromaSave Hardware Mods & Mounts

Welcome to the hardware repository for **ChromaSave**! 

Since every 3D printer is physically unique, adapting single-head multi-color systems (like the Coprint3D ChromaHead) often requires custom mounts, purge buckets, or modified fan ducts. 

This folder contains 3D printable parts designed to help you integrate ChromaSave into your specific machine.

## 🤝 Community Contributions
We highly encourage the community to share their custom designs! If you have designed a mount, a better purge bucket, or any hardware mod for your printer that makes ChromaSave run smoother, we would love to include it here.

### How to contribute your design:
1. **Fork** this repository.
2. Navigate to the `hardware/` folder.
3. If your printer brand/model folder doesn't exist, create it (e.g., `Voron/Trident/`).
4. Upload your files. 
   * 💡 **Preferred formats:** `.step` or `.f3d` files are highly appreciated as they allow others to easily remix your design. `.stl` and `.3mf` files are also welcome.
5. Include a short text file (`notes.md`) or a picture explaining how to mount it.
6. Submit a **Pull Request (PR)**!

Thank you for making ChromaSave better for everyone!
